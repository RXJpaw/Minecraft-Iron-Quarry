# Crafting Recipes

This page lists the default recipes of Iron Quarry. Each grid shows the Crafting Table from above: every letter stands for an item, and `·` is an empty slot.

Common materials like ingots, gems, dusts, storage blocks, chests and sticks also accept the same material from other mods.

> [!NOTE]
> Modpacks often change recipes. If a recipe here doesn't work in your game, look the item up in your recipe viewer (JEI or REI). [Playing a modpack or on a server](README.md#playing-a-modpack-or-on-a-server) explains how.

## Circuit Boards

Circuit boards are the main ingredient of the mod. Each tier is made from the one before it:

Blank → Lesser → Greater / Refined → Supreme → Divine → Celestial

#### Blank Circuit Board (×16)

```
U U U
M M M
D D D
```

`U` 3× Dark Prismarine, `M` 3× Copper Ingot, `D` 3× Amethyst Shard.

#### Lesser Circuit Board (×4)

```
S P S
P M P
S P S
```

`M` 1× Blank Circuit Board, `P` 4× Copper Ingot, `S` 4× Redstone Dust.

#### Greater Circuit Board (×2)

```
P M S
M P M
S M P
```

`M` 4× Lesser Circuit Board, `P` 3× Iron Ingot, `S` 2× Prismarine Shard.

#### Refined Circuit Board (×2)

```
P M S
M P M
S M P
```

`M` 4× Lesser Circuit Board, `P` 3× Gold Ingot, `S` 2× Blaze Powder.

#### Supreme Circuit Board (×2)

```
P E S
M P M
S E P
```

`M` 2× Greater Circuit Board, `E` 2× Refined Circuit Board, `P` 3× Diamond, `S` 2× Glowstone Dust.

#### Divine Circuit Board (×2)

```
P M S
M P M
S M P
```

`M` 4× Supreme Circuit Board, `P` 3× Netherite Ingot, `S` 2× Ender Pearl.

#### Celestial Circuit Board

```
S M T
M P M
T M S
```

`M` 4× Divine Circuit Board, `P` 1× Nether Star, `S` 2× Echo Shard, `T` 2× Dragon's Breath.

## Augments

The **Silk Touch Augment** and the **Chest Looting Augment** can't be crafted. See [Augments](augments.md) for where to find them.

Filling an augment and adding capacity upgrades at a Smithing Table are explained under [Filling an augment](augments.md#filling-an-augment) and [Capacity upgrades](augments.md#capacity-upgrades).

#### Empty Augment

```
· C ·
C P C
· C ·
```

`P` 1× Item Frame, `C` 4× Greater Circuit Board.

#### Augmenting Template

